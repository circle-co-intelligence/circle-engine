FAQ

Questions

Something else on your mind? Write to us at hello@co-intelligence.online.

Is the beta available to everyone?

Not yet. We invite people in small groups, so we can support each circle well and improve the product together with them.

What happens after I apply?

We review requests by hand. If we can offer you a place, we send a personal invitation to the email address you gave us. That invitation lets you create your account.

How do my guests join?

Once you have access, your guests join free in their browser using a link or six-digit room code. Only the host needs an account.

How do speaking turns work?

The person holding the talking stick speaks while everyone else listens. Choose clockwise or counterclockwise turns, or an open round where the group chooses who speaks next.

How is our conversation handled?

Video and audio are encrypted in transit and relayed live through Cloudflare’s realtime network. The host chooses when recording starts, and the group can see the room settings.

Do I have to use AI?

No. A circle works fully without AI. Milo is an optional participant you can invite into a circle when it helps.
